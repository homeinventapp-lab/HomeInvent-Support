HomeInvent Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HomeInvent handles information when you use the HomeInvent app ("HomeInvent", "we", "us", or "our"). HomeInvent is designed as an offline-first home inventory app and uses Apple technologies, including CloudKit/iCloud, to provide optional backup and synchronization features.

1. Information you add to HomeInvent

You may choose to store information such as home and room names, item names, brands, categories, quantities, purchase details, values, warranty information, notes, barcodes or QR-related information, receipts, and photos. This information is used to provide the inventory features you request.

2. On-device processing

HomeInvent uses Apple frameworks and on-device technologies for supported features such as text recognition, image/product recognition, natural-language processing, and local data management. These features are designed to process applicable information on your device rather than requiring a third-party remote AI service.

3. iCloud and CloudKit

HomeInvent can use Apple's iCloud/CloudKit services to synchronize or back up supported HomeInvent data so that you can restore or use it on your devices. Depending on your use of the app, this may include inventory records, room/home information, photos, receipts, and related data.

CloudKit private database data is associated with your iCloud account and is controlled through Apple's iCloud services. HomeInvent does not operate a separate developer-owned database that is intended to provide us with access to your private CloudKit inventory records.

Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies. HomeInvent cannot control Apple's operation or availability of iCloud services.

4. Information we collect

HomeInvent is designed not to collect your private inventory content through a developer-operated analytics or advertising system. We do not use HomeInvent to sell your inventory information or use your inventory content for advertising.

If a future version introduces additional services or data practices, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those practices become applicable.

5. Analytics and advertising

The current HomeInvent app is designed without third-party advertising SDKs and without third-party analytics services. We do not use your inventory information for targeted advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

Inventory information stored locally can be deleted through the app or by deleting app data as supported by iOS. Information synchronized to iCloud/CloudKit may remain in your iCloud storage according to Apple's services and your account settings. You are responsible for maintaining backups of information you want to preserve.

7. Children's privacy

HomeInvent is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal contact information from children.

8. Security

We use Apple's platform and CloudKit capabilities and reasonable app design practices to help protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Third-party services

HomeInvent uses Apple system frameworks and Apple services required for app functionality. Apple's handling of information through services such as iCloud is governed by Apple's own policies and terms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HomeInvent's features or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, use the contact information provided in the HomeInvent App Store listing. Please do not include passwords, Apple Account credentials, payment card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information in support requests.

Important: This page is a product-specific privacy-policy draft for the HomeInvent architecture described above. Before publishing it, verify that the production build, CloudKit configuration, support process, and App Store Connect privacy disclosures exactly match these statements.
