01 • REPORTS

One card. The policy for this home.

Insurance lives with a specific home — not a global mix of every property. Open Reports, tap Insurance, and you land on the policy attached to that home.

  • Status, type, and expiration at a glance
  • Attention count when inventory and policy disagree
  • Separate from the Insurance Claim Package workflow

Dashboard — files, status, Home Match, Evidence

Jump to Coverage, Evidence, Documents, or Policy

02 • DASHBOARD

The workspace for this policy.

Upload PDFs or scans. HomeInvent classifies what it can — declarations, contracts, schedules — and uses those files for Home Match. Ask My Policy stays one tap away.

  • Multiple files on one policy, with a short description of each
  • Home Match compares recorded inventory value with identified limits
  • Evidence shows how many high-value items already have a photo and receipt
  • Reading happens on this device. No third-party cloud AI.
03 • REVIEW

See where the numbers differ.

If recorded jewelry or personal property is higher than a limit found in the upload, HomeInvent flags it for review — and links the source page so you can read the wording yourself.

  • “Needs attention” is a comparison, not a coverage decision
  • Policy amount and home amount side by side
  • Open and highlight the passage in the original file
Language HomeInvent says what it found in your documents. It does not say you are covered, underinsured, or that a claim will be paid.
04 • COVERAGE, LIMITS, EXCLUSIONS

Every finding traces back to a page.

Coverage, Limits, and Exclusions are three views of the same upload. Tap a source chip to open the PDF at the line HomeInvent used.

05 • EVIDENCE

Know what a claim would still need.

Photos, receipts, serial numbers, and high-value items are counted against this home. Finish the gaps now — before you ever need a claim package.

  • Progress for photos, receipts, serials, and high-value items
  • A list of items still missing a photo or receipt
  • This prepares evidence. It is not a claim filing tool.
06 • ASK MY POLICY

Ask in plain language. Read the passage.

Type or speak a question. HomeInvent searches the uploaded files, answers from what it finds, and lists matching passages — including “Is water damage covered?” and “What is my personal property limit?”

  • Suggested questions so you do not have to start from scratch
  • Voice search when microphone and speech access are allowed
  • Answers stay cautious when the documents are incomplete
ON THIS DEVICE
