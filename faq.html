FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common HomeInvent questions.

Does HomeInvent work offline?

Yes. HomeInvent is designed as an offline-first app and core inventory features can work without an internet connection. Some services, such as iCloud synchronization, require connectivity.

Does HomeInvent upload my inventory to a HomeInvent server?

HomeInvent does not operate a separate developer-owned server for your private CloudKit inventory records. If you enable iCloud/CloudKit features, supported data is stored through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud storage.

Can I restore my inventory on another device?

Supported HomeInvent data can be synchronized or restored through iCloud/CloudKit when the appropriate iCloud account and app configuration are available. Keep your old device or its data intact until you have confirmed the new device has restored successfully.

Does HomeInvent process photos using a remote AI service?

HomeInvent is designed to use Apple on-device technologies for supported recognition features. It does not require a third-party remote AI service for those core recognition functions.

Can I edit information detected by scanning?

Yes. Detected information should be reviewed and can be corrected manually before or after saving, depending on the feature and app version.

Can I store receipts?

Yes. You can keep supported receipt images and related information with inventory records. If iCloud backup/sync is enabled, supported images may be stored in your private iCloud/CloudKit storage.

Does HomeInvent track my location?

HomeInvent does not need your GPS location to organize items by rooms. A room such as Kitchen, Bedroom, or Garage is an inventory organization label, not GPS location data.

Does HomeInvent contain advertising?

HomeInvent is designed without in-app advertising.

What if an item is not recognized?

You can add or correct the item's information manually. Recognition is designed to assist entry and may not identify every product or brand.

What should I do if iCloud sync is not working?

Check that your device is signed in to iCloud, that iCloud services are available, and that HomeInvent has the required iCloud permissions. Keep the app open and connected long enough for synchronization to complete. Avoid deleting local data until you have confirmed that your backup is available.

How do I contact support?

Use the support contact information provided on the HomeInvent App Store listing. Please include useful troubleshooting details but never send passwords or payment information.
