Everything you need to know about HomeInvent.

Does HomeInvent work offline?

Yes. HomeInvent is designed as an offline-first app and core inventory features can work without an internet connection. Services such as iCloud synchronization require connectivity.

Does HomeInvent upload my inventory to a HomeInvent server?

HomeInvent does not operate a separate developer-owned server for your private CloudKit inventory records. If iCloud and CloudKit are enabled, supported data is stored through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud storage.

Can I restore my inventory on another device?

Supported HomeInvent data can be synchronized or restored through iCloud and CloudKit when the appropriate iCloud account and app configuration are available.

Does HomeInvent use remote AI?

HomeInvent is designed to use Apple on-device technologies for supported recognition features and does not require a third-party remote AI service for those core recognition functions.

Can I edit information detected by scanning?

Yes. Detected information should be reviewed and can be corrected manually.

Can I store receipts?

Yes. You can keep supported receipt images and related information with inventory records. With iCloud backup and sync enabled, supported images may be stored in your private iCloud and CloudKit storage.

Does HomeInvent track my location?

HomeInvent does not need GPS location to organize items by rooms. Labels such as Kitchen, Bedroom or Garage are inventory organization labels, not GPS location data.

Does HomeInvent contain advertising?

HomeInvent is designed without in-app advertising.

What if an item isn't recognized?

You can add or correct its information manually. Recognition is designed to assist entry and may not identify every product or brand.

What if iCloud sync isn't working?

Check that you're signed in to iCloud, iCloud services are available, and HomeInvent has the required iCloud permissions. Keep the app open and connected long enough for synchronization to complete. Avoid deleting local data until you've confirmed the backup is available.
