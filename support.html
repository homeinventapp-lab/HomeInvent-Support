01 • GETTING STARTED

Build your inventory

HomeInvent lets you organize belongings by home, room and item. Create your home and rooms, then add the items you want to keep track of.

Add an item

  1. Open your inventory and choose the add-item option.
  2. Enter or scan available product information.
  3. Add photos, purchase details, value, warranty information and notes as needed.
  4. Save the item and place it in the appropriate room.
02 • SCANNING

Capture information faster

HomeInvent can use supported Apple on-device technologies for camera capture, barcode and QR scanning, text recognition and product or object recognition.

If recognition is incorrect

Review detected information before saving. You can edit fields manually. Recognition is designed to assist entry and may not identify every product or brand perfectly.

Tip Good lighting, a clear label and a steady camera generally improve recognition.
03 • RECEIPTS & WARRANTIES

Keep proof with the item

Attach supported item photos and receipt images to your inventory records. Keep warranty details connected to the product they belong to.

04 • SEARCH

Find things naturally

Use normal search or supported natural-language queries to find items and related information. If a query doesn't return the expected result, try a shorter query or search by item, brand, room or category.

05 • iCLOUD BACKUP

Your inventory can follow you.

HomeInvent can use Apple's CloudKit and iCloud services to preserve supported inventory data in your private iCloud account, helping you restore or access supported data on your devices.

Before moving to a new device

  • Make sure you're signed in to iCloud.
  • Make sure HomeInvent has the required iCloud access.
  • Allow synchronization to complete before deleting HomeInvent from the old device.
  • Use the same iCloud account on the new device when restoring.
Important Cloud synchronization depends on Apple's iCloud availability, account state, connectivity and the app's CloudKit configuration.
06 • CONTACT
