How can we help?

Find answers about inventory, scanning, receipts, warranties, search, and iCloud backup.

Getting Started

HomeInvent lets you organize your belongings by home, room, and item. Start by creating your home and rooms, then add the items you want to keep track of.

Add an item

  1. Open the inventory and choose the option to add an item.
  2. Enter or scan the available product information.
  3. Add photos, purchase details, value, warranty information, notes, and other details as needed.
  4. Save the item and place it in the appropriate room.

Scanning

HomeInvent can use supported Apple on-device technologies for camera-based capture, barcode/QR scanning, text recognition, and product/object recognition.

If recognition is incorrect

Review the detected information before saving. You can edit any field manually. Recognition is intended to speed up entry and may not identify every product or brand perfectly.

Good lighting, a clear label, and a steady camera generally improve recognition.

Receipts & Photos

You can attach item photos and supported receipt images to your inventory records. Keep important documentation with the item it belongs to so it is easier to find later.

Photos stay under your control

HomeInvent is designed to process supported recognition features on-device. If iCloud backup/sync is enabled, supported HomeInvent data and images may be stored in your private iCloud/CloudKit storage.

Warranties

Add warranty information to the relevant item, including dates and notes supported by the app. Keeping warranty details with the item makes coverage easier to review.

Search

Use HomeInvent search to find items and related information. Natural-language search can help you express what you are looking for in everyday language when supported by your installed version.

If a query does not return the expected result, try a shorter query or search using the item's name, brand, room, category, or another saved detail.

iCloud Backup & Restore

HomeInvent can use Apple's CloudKit/iCloud services to preserve supported inventory data in the user's private iCloud account. This can help you restore or access supported data on another device signed in to the appropriate iCloud account.

Before moving to a new device

  • Make sure you are signed in to iCloud.
  • Make sure HomeInvent has the required iCloud/CloudKit access.
  • Allow synchronization to complete before deleting HomeInvent from the old device.
  • Keep the same iCloud account available on the new device when restoring.

Cloud synchronization depends on Apple's iCloud availability, account state, device connectivity, and the app's current CloudKit configuration.

Privacy

HomeInvent is designed as an offline-first app. Core inventory features can work without an internet connection. Optional iCloud/CloudKit storage is used to support backup and restoration across the user's devices.

HomeInvent does not operate a separate developer-owned inventory database for your private CloudKit records. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
